**Unlucky Client 2.5** — MC 26.3 / Fabric. 191 modules.

**Combat**
- Added ElytraMace — you fly and aim the dive; it swaps the elytra for a chestplate on the last tick that still reaches, lands a real mace smash once the server has ended the glide, then puts the elytra back on, glides again and fires a rocket once the way ahead is clear. It only commits to a dive whose miss you would survive or that a re-glide or a wind charge under your feet can catch. The Inventory chestplate source keeps the critical hit; Offhand and Hotbar swap by right-click, which resets the attack charge on 26.3 servers, so their smash lands without the crit.
- Fixed module attacks swinging only on your screen. 26.3 no longer sends a swing with the arm, so Aura, TriggerBot, CrystalAura, AnchorAura, MaceCombo, AutoClicker, Criticals and Surround now send it with the hit, like a real click, and use the weapon's own attack swing.

**Player**
- Fixed AutoEat getting stuck on food it could not eat. With a full hunger bar it now only reaches for food you can eat anyway, such as golden apples, instead of holding right-click on a steak forever.
- Fixed AntiAFK's swing not counting as activity on 26.3 servers; it now sends the swing the server's idle timer listens for.
- Fixed GhostHand ignoring the new straw beds.

**World**
- Fixed Nuker, SpeedMine and VeinMiner breaking without a visible swing for other players.
- Fixed the Packet swing mode of Scaffold, Surround, HoleFill, AutoTrap, Burrow, LiquidFiller and SpawnProofer sending an attack swing on 26.3, which reset your attack charge on every block. The server now shows placement swings to others by itself, so Packet places without one.

**ClickGUI**
- Fixed typing on 26.3. No letter reached the ClickGUI search in either style, the block, item and brew pickers, text settings, Configs, Console, Friends or the HUD editor's text fields; backspace and the arrows worked, which is why it looked half-broken. 26.3 only delivers typed characters to screens that ask for them, and ours now do.
- Fixed the config Import/Export and skin file pickers: the file-type filter could come up garbled and the window had no title.

**Render**
- Fixed RainbowEnchant colouring the whole item instead of its glint. 26.3 draws the glint inside the item itself, so the tint now goes on the glint texture: the item keeps its own colours and the glint takes yours, at vanilla's strength in every hue. Hide glint and Opacity work as before.

**Chat**
- Fixed `.bind` with number keys: every digit but 0 bound an unrelated key, such as Enter or Escape.
- Fixed binds on numpad divide, minus and period from older configs; they now carry over instead of being cleared.
