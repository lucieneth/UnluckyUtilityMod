**Unlucky Client 2.4.1** — MC 26.3 / Fabric. 190 modules.

**Fixes**
- Fixed mouse buttons in the ClickGUI and every other client screen on 26.3. Left click was running right-click actions and right click did nothing, because 26.3 renumbered the mouse buttons along with the keys. Toggling modules, expanding settings, dragging panels, the pickers and the HUD editor's widget menu now respond to the correct button.
- Fixed setting keybinds in the ClickGUI. The bind row was waiting for a left click that 26.3 no longer reported as one. Binds you already had kept working; only setting new ones was broken.
