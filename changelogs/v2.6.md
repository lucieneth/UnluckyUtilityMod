**Unlucky Client 2.6** — MC 26.3 / Fabric. 208 modules.

**Combat**
- Added BedAura — places beds against a target and sets them off wherever beds explode. It reads the bed rule at the bed's own position, so datapack dimensions work too, and it waits for AnchorAura in the End so the two don't fight over the hotbar.
- Added AutoCity — mines one block of a target's surround, preferring the side that opens a crystal spot at their feet.
- Added SelfTrap — covers your head (Top), your head ring (Face) or your whole hole (Full), so nothing lands a crystal or an anchor on top of you.
- Added AutoWeb — drops cobwebs right where the target is standing.
- Added ShieldBreaker — knocks a raised shield down with one quick axe hit the moment it points at you, then hands your weapon back.
- Added AutoWeapon — puts the best weapon in hand at the moment of a hit, whoever made it: your own clicks, Aura or anything else. The slot reaches the server together with the hit.
- Added Quiver — shoots tipped arrows straight up so Strength and Speed come down on you. BowAimbot leaves those shots alone.
- Surround and AutoTrap have a new Custom shape, drawn block by block in a 3D pattern editor with presets and layers.
- Aura, MaceCombo and AutoXPRepair now share the hotbar instead of swapping items back under each other.

**Player**
- Added NoMissDelay — removes vanilla's half-second attack lockout after a swing at nothing.
- Added MiddleClickAction — middle-click throws a pearl, rocket, wind charge or XP bottle from the hotbar without switching to it. Middle-clicking a player still adds a friend.
- Added AutoOminousBottle — drinks an ominous bottle whenever Bad Omen runs out, for raid and trial farms.
- AutoXPRepair goes through the offhand manager, so AutoTotem always wins the offhand, and during an Aura fight it gives the sword back.
- Capes has a live preview of the selected cape on your skin.

**Movement**
- Added Timer — runs the game faster or slower than 20 ticks a second.

**World**
- Added ChestManager — a searchable record of your base's chests. `.cm add` tags a chest, `.cm click` tags everything you right-click, and `.cm search <item>` makes every chest holding the item glow, shulkers and bundles inside them included, with the count above it and the slot outlined once you open it. Scanning happens without a window ever opening on your screen. The ClickGUI has a Chests tab with a 3D view of your tagged chests.
- Added Excavator — digs out the box between `.excavate pos1` and `pos2`, top down, one layer at a time.
- Added HighwayBuilder — digs and paves a straight highway in any of the eight directions. It never walks onto a floor that isn't paved yet, and it stops with a reason when it runs out of blocks or pickaxes, or reaches lava or bedrock.
- Excavator, HighwayBuilder and Printer can show their work area in 3D, with finished and remaining blocks and how far along the job is.

**Render**
- Added AntiInvis — draws invisible entities see-through, like an invisible teammate.
- Added FocusFps — caps the frame rate while the game window isn't focused.

**HUD**
- Added the CrystalMap widget — the crystal target's model, health and armour, above a map of every spot a crystal could go around them, coloured by how much it hurts them compared with you. The map can be 3D or flat from the top, and players on it are drawn as their heads.
- The Radar draws the terrain from above like a vanilla map, and players as their heads. Both can be turned off.
- PlayerModel shows flying, swimming and crawling the way the inventory does: the body lies flat, the wings spread, and the box turns sideways to fit it without zooming in.

**Misc**
- Added Notifier — toasts for players entering or leaving visual range, totem pops and armour wearing thin.

**ClickGUI**
- New Preview tab in both styles: a live model of you with the render modules that change your look, and a small block scene that shows BlockOutline, BreakIndicators, HoleESP, VoidESP and LightOverlay exactly as they are set.

**Chat**
- New commands: `.hclip` (clip forward), `.drop` (hand, hotbar or the whole inventory), `.peek` (look inside the held shulker without placing it), `.server` (lists the server's plugins), `.fov`, `.macro`, `.give` and `.enchant` (creative only; `.enchant` takes any level from 1 to 255), plus `.excavate` and `.cm`.
